Follow the workout

  1. Choose your exercise time, rest time, and number of rounds.
  2. Start with a three-second “Get ready” countdown, then alternate exercise and rest.
  3. Finish after the last exercise, with no final rest.

Short ticks mark 3, 2, 1 before starting and the final five seconds of exercise and rest. A stronger beep marks each phase change; a longer, lower tone marks completion.

For phases of five seconds or less, the opening beep replaces the first tick. Pause holds your place, including during “Get ready”. Resume continues without repeating the starting countdown.

Reset returns to the beginning using your chosen settings. Refreshing the page restores the defaults.
